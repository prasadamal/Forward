## Full description (Play, also the App Store description)

```
Forward anything. Find it sorted. Keep it safe.

You find a Bangalore food vlog on YouTube, a café on Instagram and an app recommendation on X.
Share each one to Forward. All three land in Bangalore, filed under Food, Places to Visit and
Apps & Tech, no matter which app they came from.

SHARE FROM ANY APP
Links, text, photos, GIFs, videos and files arrive through the normal share sheet. Up to 10
images at once.

SORTED FOR YOU, ON YOUR PHONE
Forward recognises about 280 cities, states and countries, 300 neighbourhoods, landmarks and
treks, and 13 topics such as Food, Places to Visit, Events, Shopping, Movies & Music and Memes.
Sorting happens on your phone. No AI service, no account.

FOLDERS AS DEEP AS YOU LIKE
Nest folders without limit, keep an item in several folders, and let any folder collect items
that mention its keywords.

A PRIVATE VAULT
Everything is stored in an AES-256 encrypted database on your phone. Lock it with a 6-digit PIN
or a password, plus Face ID or fingerprint. Screenshots are blocked and the app-switcher
preview is blurred.

CARDS, LOGINS AND SECRET NOTES
Keep cards, passwords and secure notes in the same vault. Secrets load only when you open them
and confirm it's you, and a copied secret is cleared from the clipboard after 45 seconds.

YOURS ONLY
No account, no server, no analytics, no ads. Backups are one encrypted file that you keep
wherever you like.

Good to know: Instagram and Facebook don't show post captions to other apps. When Forward can't
read a post it tells you, and a word or two ("Bangalore café") is enough to file it.
```
