1. THE IDEA
-----------
"Forward anything. Find it sorted. Keep it safe."
You share things INTO Forward from any app (YouTube, Instagram, X, WhatsApp, Photos, Files,
browsers) and it files them by place and topic, on your phone, in an encrypted vault.
Example: a Bangalore food vlog, a place on Instagram and an app on X all land under Bangalore
(Food / Places to Visit / Apps & Tech). It also holds cards, passwords and secret notes.
No account, no server, no analytics. It cures "I saved it somewhere".


2. THE END PRODUCT
------------------
The private "second brain" for things people save from social media: share it in, find it by
city and topic, get reminded, plan trips from saved places. Android first, then iPhone.
Monetisation later: a one-time Pro unlock (OCR, map view, device-to-device sync), not a
subscription, and not at launch.


3. FEATURES THAT EXIST TODAY (all merged to main)
--------------------------------------------------
- Share intake from any app (iOS Share Extension, Android share intents; up to 10 images).
- Offline classifier: ~280 cities/states/countries + ~300 neighbourhoods/landmarks/treks,
  13 topics; reads titles, captions, hashtags, URL slugs; whole-word matching; India-biased.
- When Instagram hides a caption, it asks for a word or two to file it.
- Nested folders, items in several folders, smart auto-collect folders.
- Wallet: cards (Luhn/expiry checks), logins with password generator, secure notes; clipboard
  clears after 45 s.
- Lock: PIN/password, Face ID/fingerprint, auto-lock, screenshot blocking, blurred app switcher.
- SQLCipher AES-256 vault; encrypted backup file; optional link previews (can be off).
- Share a folder as a list of links (private items never included) — PR #16.
- Reminders on saved items (Later today ... In a month; notification never shows the item;
  "Reminders" section on home; included in backups) — PR #18.
- WCAG AA contrast pass — PR #17.
- Store docs in store/ (listing copy, privacy-form answers, device test); PRIVACY.md hosted on a
  free Render static site; CI.


4. ON HOLD / IDEAS (not built)

opening the app; on-device OCR for screenshots/reel covers (fixes the Instagram gap);
map and city list of saved places; shareable collections as a link/image; resurfacing

7. MARKETING
------------
- Who: 18-35 in Indian cities who save food/travel/shopping reels and lose them; privacy-minded
  note-takers.
- Headline: "Save a reel from any app. Find it by city." Trust line: "No account. No AI service.
  Encrypted on your phone." Store title like "Forward: Save Reels & Links".
- Channels: 20-second screen-recording reels ("I saved 40 Goa reels, here is how I find them"),
  Reddit city subs (r/bangalore, r/Kochi, r/mumbai) with genuinely useful lists, Product Hunt +
  Indie Hackers for the privacy angle, travel creators ("trip folder").
- Launch: week 0 internal test with 20 friends; week 1 Play release + Product Hunt + 3 videos;
  weeks 2-6 two videos and one Reddit list a week; iOS when the Apple account is paid.
- Targets (90 days): 1,000 installs, 4.5+ rating, 25% save 3+ items in week one, 10% open a
  folder weekly. Likely failure: people save a dozen things and never reopen it — watch saves
  per user in week two.
