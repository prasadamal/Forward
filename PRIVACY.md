# Forward privacy policy

**Effective date:** [date you publish]
**Applies to:** the Forward app for iPhone and Android, published by [your name or studio name].

## The short version

Forward does not collect your personal data. Everything you save stays on your phone, in an
encrypted vault. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We,
the people who make Forward, can't see anything you save.

## What Forward stores, and where

Everything you add to Forward stays on your phone, inside an encrypted database (AES-256,
SQLCipher). That includes links, notes, images, videos and files, folders, cards, logins and
secure notes. The encryption key is protected by your passcode and by your phone's secure
hardware (the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore). None of it is sent to us.

## What leaves your phone, and when

- **Link previews (optional).** When you save a link, Forward asks for the page's title,
  description and thumbnail so it can name and sort the item. The request goes to the site
  itself, its image server, or that platform's public preview service (YouTube and X). It
  works like opening the link in a browser: that site sees your IP address and the link.
  Their privacy policies apply to those requests. You can turn previews off in
  Settings › Link previews.
- **Things you choose to send.** If you share an item out, open it in another app, or export
  an encrypted backup, it goes where you send it.
- **Phone backups.** Android app backups are turned off for Forward. iPhone backups to iCloud
  or a computer may include Forward's encrypted database. That copy can't be opened on another
  phone, because the key stays in this phone's secure hardware. Use Forward's own encrypted
  backup to move to a new phone.

## Permissions

Forward does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or storage.
You choose photos and files in the system pickers, which pass on only what you pick. If you
turn on Face ID or fingerprint unlock, your phone does the check; Forward never receives
biometric data.

## What we receive

Nothing from the app itself. Apple and Google give developers download numbers, and crash
reports from people who allowed sharing with developers in their phone settings. Those are
governed by Apple's and Google's privacy policies.

## Children

Forward collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Delete items and empty the trash, or use Settings › Erase vault to delete everything at once.
Deleting the app also removes its data from your phone. Backup files you exported are yours to
delete.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
